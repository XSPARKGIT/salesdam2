Approval Processes

A step-by-step script for testing BRD Section 8. Approval processes are already set up on a Claim object in the My Company workspace — you are checking that records are routed to the right people, in order, that each stage does what the process says, and that a submitter can take a request back.

What is already in place

  • Claim object (Claims in the Insurance sidebar): Claim Reference (CLM-00001 …), Claim Number, Claim Amount, Claim Status (Draft, Pending Approval, Approved, Rejected), Assigned Approver, Rejection Reason, Approval Note.
  • Claim Manager Review — amount above the company threshold (CONFIG("approvals.threshold_amount"), set to R1 000); one step: the owner\'s manager; locks while pending; sets Claim Status at each stage.
  • Claim High Value Review — amount above R5 000; step 1 System Admin by name, step 2 anyone holding the Claims Manager role; records the approver on the claim; locks; on approval starts the flow Claim Approval Stamp and stores the comment in Approval Note; rejection needs a reason, stored in Rejection Reason.
  • Claim Referral Review — amount above R100 000; step 1 the person in Assigned Approver, step 2 the Claims Panel group; does not lock.
  • People for the second-person tests: Sam Submitter (role Claims Handler, manager System Admin) and Alex Approver (role Claims Manager, in Claims Panel). Claims Handler sits below Claims Manager in the role hierarchy. These two test accounts have been switched off; ask your administrator to reactivate them, or use two of your own colleagues given the same roles.

Things worth knowing before you begin

  • Pages take a while to open. The application runs on your machine but its database is in the cloud, so a screen can take ten to thirty seconds to fill in.
  • Approved or rejected claims are finished with their process but remain ordinary records; they can be deleted in the tidy-up (Test 22).
  • Tests that change a setting put it back: Test 3 moves the approval threshold and returns it; Test 8 clears and restores Sam\'s manager; Test 5 switches a process off and on.
  • Test claims are named M8-1 … M8-9. Claim References (CLM-0000x) are numbered automatically, so use the Claim Number to find them.
  • The earlier fixtures (POL-00001, POL-00003, POL-00004) are never submitted by this script.

Still to be covered

These points sit outside this script:

  • Emails. Whether an approval, rejection or submission email is delivered to a real inbox. The content and recipients are proven by the automated approval checks, which capture mail instead of sending it.
  • Lead Approval. The built-in Lead-to-Deal process exists only where the Lead object does; it is proven by the automated checks (Test 21).
  • Drag and drop of the step order. Steps are reordered with the move up / move down buttons.
  • Reopening an approved or rejected claim. A rejected claim can be edited and submitted again; an approved one is finished.
  • System writes. Internal platform writes (for example the Insurance module moving a policy through its lifecycle) do not check the approval lock; only the screen, the record API, bulk delete and merge do.
  • Whether the person who submitted may also approve their own request. They may when they are also the named approver (Test 6 used System Admin for both). The BRD does not require separation, so none is enforced.
  • Import. Importing rows creates new records only and cannot overwrite a locked one.

Reporting a problem

For anything that does not match the expected result, note the test number, what you saw instead, and the record you were working on. A screenshot of the screen as it looked when it went wrong is usually enough for us to reproduce it.
