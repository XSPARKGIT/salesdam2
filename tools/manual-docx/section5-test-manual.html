What has already been set up for you

  • Insurance — the default App. Its objects, in order: Policies, Insured Lives. It opens on Policies.
  • Broker Desk — a second App, described as Where brokers place and track policies. Its objects, in order: Brokers, Policies. It opens on Brokers.
  • Policies is shared: it is a tab in both Apps. Insured Lives belongs to Insurance only, and Brokers to Broker Desk only.
  • Broker Desk Access — a Permission Set that gives its users the Broker Desk App. Nobody is assigned to it yet.

Two things worth knowing before you begin

  • Pages take a while to open. This environment runs the application on your machine but keeps its database in the cloud, so a screen can take ten to thirty seconds to fill in — and a list can look empty before it does. Give a page time before deciding it is stuck. After switching Apps, allow a few seconds for the left-hand navigation to settle.
  • Tests that change something put it back. Where a test says don't save, reload the page afterwards; where it saves a change, its last step undoes it.

Part 1 — Defining an App

BRD §5.1. Apps are managed under Setup → Workspaces.

  1. Go to Setup → Workspaces. Find the Broker Desk card.
  2. Click Quick Edit and look at the fields. Click Cancel.
  3. Click Open on the card and look at the Settings tab.
Expected The card shows the name and the description Where brokers place and track policies. Quick Edit offers Name, Description, an icon picker and a colour. The Settings tab adds a Default landing page and a default workspace option.
  1. On Setup → Workspaces click New Workspace.
  2. Type the name Test App, choose any icon and colour, and click Create.
  3. Open the App switcher (the App name at the top of the left-hand panel).
  4. Back on Setup → Workspaces, click Delete on the Test App card and confirm.
Expected A Test App card appears in the list and Test App is offered in the App switcher. It starts with no objects chosen. Deleting it asks you to confirm — This cannot be undone — and removes it from the list and the switcher.
  1. Open the Broker Desk App and go to its Objects tab.
  2. Look at the highlighted objects and at the Tab order panel under them.
  3. Click Move down beside Brokers. Don't save — reload the page.
Expected Two objects are highlighted — Broker and Policy — and the footer says 2 objects selected. Tab order lists Brokers, Policies. After Move down it reads Policies, Brokers; after the reload it is back to Brokers, Policies.
  1. Open the App switcher and choose Insurance. Read the object names in the left-hand panel from top to bottom.
  2. Switch to Broker Desk and read them again.
Expected Insurance shows Policies, Insured Lives. Broker Desk shows Brokers, Policies. Policies is first in one App and second in the other: the order belongs to the App, not to the object.
  1. In Insurance, click Policies and open POL-00001.
  2. Switch to Broker Desk, click Policies and open POL-00001 again.
Expected Policies is available in both Apps and it is the very same object: the same list, the same POL-00001, the same layout. Nothing was copied for the second App.
  1. In Broker Desk, look for Insured Lives in the left-hand panel.
  2. Change the end of the address in your browser to /o/insured_life and press Enter.
Expected Insured Lives is not in Broker Desk's navigation. Opening it by address shows This object isn't available — it isn't part of the "Broker Desk" workspace, or you don't have permission to view it, with a Go to Home link.
  1. In Setup → Workspaces → Broker Desk → Objects, click the Insured Life card and click Save Changes.
  2. Switch to Broker Desk if you are not already in it, and click the new Insured Lives tab.
  3. Go back to the Objects tab, click the Insured Life card again to remove it, and click Save Changes.
Expected Insured Lives joins the end of the Broker Desk navigation straight away and opens with all six of its records, with the layouts, validation and views it already had. Nothing about the object was touched. Removing it puts Broker Desk back as it was.

Part 2 — Moving between Apps

BRD §5.2. The App switcher is the App name at the top of the left-hand panel.

  1. Click the App name at the top of the left-hand panel (it says Insurance or Broker Desk).
Expected A list headed Workspaces shows Insurance and Broker Desk, with a Manage Workspaces link underneath. The App you are in is the one named at the top.
  1. Switch to Insurance and wait a few seconds. Note the objects in the left-hand panel.
  2. Switch to Broker Desk, wait, and note them again.
Expected The panel shows only the open App's objects — Policies and Insured Lives for Insurance; Brokers and Policies for Broker Desk. Objects from the other App are not offered.
  1. Switch to Insurance. Note the page you land on.
  2. Switch to Broker Desk. Note the page you land on.
  3. Open Setup → Workspaces → Broker Desk → Settings and read Default landing page.
Expected Switching to Insurance opens Policies; switching to Broker Desk opens Brokers. The Settings tab shows the matching Default landing page for each App.
  1. Open Setup → Workspaces → Insurance → Settings. Set Default landing page to Home and click Save Settings.
  2. Switch to Broker Desk, then switch back to Insurance.
  3. Return to Insurance's Settings, set the landing page back to Policy, and save.
Expected With Home chosen, switching into Insurance opens the Dashboard rather than Policies. After you set it back to Policy, Insurance opens on Policies again.
  1. Open Setup → Workspaces → Broker Desk → Settings.
  2. Open the Default landing page list and read its choices. Don't change anything.
Expected The choices are None (stay on current page), Home, Broker and Policy. Insured Life is not offered, because it is not part of Broker Desk.

Part 3 — Who sees which App

BRD §5.3. Access is set under Setup → Roles and Setup → Permission Sets.

  1. Go to Setup → Permission Sets.
  2. Find Broker Desk Access and click the pencil Edit icon on its row. Look under Apps granted, then click Cancel.
Expected The page explains that a user's App switcher shows apps from their role, their permission sets, and any app they were added to directly. Broker Desk Access shows 0 users, so nobody is affected yet. Its Edit window has Broker Desk ticked under Apps granted.
  1. Go to Setup → Roles and click Edit role on the Standard User card.
  2. Open its App Access tab. Don't change anything.
Expected Insurance (default — everyone) is ticked and locked, because the default App is open to every user. Broker Desk is unticked, so Standard Users do not see it unless a Permission Set or direct membership gives it to them.
  1. On the Standard User role's App Access tab, read the sentence under the heading.
  2. Read the sentence under the heading on Setup → Permission Sets.
Expected Both say the same thing: App access is separate from object permissions. A role can read an object — for example in a report — without the App that contains it, and an App only ever shows the objects its user is allowed to read.
Checked by the platform too This environment has a single Super User, so the other roles cannot be signed in as. The behaviour has been proven in two automated suites: App access (26 checks — including that a user without the App can still report on Policy data, and cannot switch into an App they were not granted) and workspace isolation (31 checks).

Still to be covered

These points sit outside this script:

  • Seeing the App switcher as another user. Only a Super User account exists here, so what a Standard User, Read Only or Permission-Set user is offered is covered by the platform's own checks rather than by signing in as each.
  • The delete confirmation. It uses your browser's own prompt, which an automated run cannot answer; it is for you to confirm by hand in Test 2.

Reporting a problem

For anything that does not match the expected result, note the test number, what you saw instead, and the App or object you were working in. A screenshot of the screen as it looked when it went wrong is usually enough for us to reproduce it.
